Open any file ending in `.mk.md` (an ordinary text file; create one if you like), then open the Markii preview one of three ways:

- The preview icon in the editor's title bar.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> on macOS).
- **Markii: Open Preview** from the Command Palette.

The preview opens beside the editor, follows the active `.mk.md` file, and updates as you type.
